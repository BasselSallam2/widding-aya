Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Egypt Stitch & Tex?

Egypt Stitch & Tex is an international B2B exhibition held annually through two main editions covering the textile and ready-made garment manufacturing industries.

Textile Technologies Edition: The 24th International Exhibition for Spinning, Weaving, Carpet and Fabric Machinery, Printing Technologies and Accessories — covering spinning, weaving, knitting, carpet machinery, dyeing and finishing, textile printing, inspection and QC, automation and software, yarns and fabrics, dyes/inks/chemicals, and spare parts.

Garment Machinery Edition: The 22nd International Exhibition for Garment Manufacturing Machinery, Printing Technologies and Accessories — covering industrial sewing, cutting and spreading, embroidery, fusing and ironing, finishing, garment printing, design systems, packaging, and garment accessories.

The exhibition connects manufacturers, machinery suppliers, factory owners, production managers, engineers, buyers, and industry professionals.

2. What are the official names of the two Egypt Stitch & Tex editions?

The first edition is The 24th International Exhibition for Spinning, Weaving, Carpet and Fabric Machinery, Printing Technologies and Accessories. The second edition is The 22nd International Exhibition for Garment Manufacturing Machinery, Printing Technologies and Accessories.

3. How often is Egypt Stitch & Tex held?

Each Egypt Stitch & Tex edition is held once every year: one edition for textile machinery and technologies and another for ready-made garment manufacturing machinery.

4. When is the Egypt Stitch & Tex Textile Technologies Edition 2026?

The Textile Technologies Edition will take place from Thursday, 24 September to Sunday, 27 September 2026.

5. What are the exhibition’s opening hours?

The exhibition is open daily from 11:00 AM to 8:30 PM throughout the show.

6. Where is the Textile Technologies Edition held?

It is held at the Cairo International Convention Centre (CICC) in Nasr City, Cairo, Egypt.

7. How long does the exhibition last?

The Textile Technologies Edition lasts four days, giving visitors enough time to inspect machinery, compare suppliers, and attend business meetings.

8. Who organizes Egypt Stitch & Tex?

Egypt Stitch & Tex is co-organized by Vision Fairs and Business Plus Fairs, organizers of specialized B2B trade exhibitions.

9. Is Egypt Stitch & Tex a specialized trade exhibition?

Yes. Egypt Stitch & Tex is a specialized B2B trade exhibition for textile, garment, printing, and manufacturing professionals.

10. Who should visit Egypt Stitch & Tex?

The exhibition is designed for factory owners, production managers, engineers, procurement teams, importers, distributors, machinery traders, manufacturers, and international buyers.

11. Is the exhibition open to the general public?

The exhibition is primarily intended for industry professionals, business owners, buyers, and decision-makers rather than general consumer audiences.

12. Why should I visit Egypt Stitch & Tex?

You can explore new machinery, compare technologies and suppliers, discuss your factory’s requirements, and make better production and investment decisions.

It covers spinning, weaving, knitting, carpet machinery, textile printing, dyeing, finishing, inspection systems, automation, yarns, fabrics, chemicals, and production accessories.
